For parents and carers at {{ school.name }}

Your child is invited to MyPhone/MyBrain.

In the coming weeks, your child will take part in MyPhone/MyBrain at {{ school.name }}: a University of Leeds study about young people’s phones, brains and growing up. Here is what it involves, and the one thing we ask you to do online now. It takes about five minutes.

Share screen time online  → Read what it involves first

What taking part involves

  • A two-hour workshop at school with our researchers, for the classes taking part: young people learn how the brain works, and wear a soft cap that records brain activity (EEG) while they do simple computer tasks. It is safe and painless, and it cannot read thoughts.
  • Short questionnaires about everyday life, phones and how your child feels.
  • Linking, through Connected West Yorkshire, with records already held about your child (health and school records), using a code instead of a name and only with the approvals in place.
  • Only if you want to: screenshots of the phone’s screen-time summary, showing which apps are used and for how long.
  • We never read messages, look at photos or see what is posted.

You don’t need to do anything for your child to take part in the workshop. On the day they can say no to any part, or stop at any time, and it will not affect anything at school.

Share screen time online

  1. You answer a few questions about your child’s phone use, and say whether we can have their screen time.
  2. You send screenshots from your own phone, if you can see their screen time there (Apple Family Sharing or Google Family Link); or your child sends them from theirs, if they want to.
  3. Young people aged 16 or 17 can do this themselves.

Please do this as soon as you get this page: it takes about five minutes.

Don’t want your child to take part?

You can opt your child out of the workshop. That also means nothing about them is linked with health or school records, and none of their information is used. Opting out is by email, from a parent or carer; there is no form or paper slip. If you opt out after the workshop, we withdraw your child’s information too, as far as is still possible.
