For parents and carers at {{ school.name }}

Your child is invited to MyPhone/MyBrain.

{{ school.name }} is taking part in MyPhone/MyBrain, a University of Leeds study about young people’s phones, brains and growing up. Here is what it involves and how to say yes online, in about five minutes.

Start the form  → Read what it involves first

What taking part involves

  • Short surveys about everyday life, phones and how your child feels.
  • For some young people, a session at school with our researchers: a soft cap records brain activity (EEG) while they do simple computer tasks. It is safe and painless, and it cannot read thoughts.
  • Only if you and your child want to: screenshots of the phone’s screen-time summary, showing which apps are used and for how long.
  • We never read messages, look at photos or see what is posted.

Your child can only take part if you give permission and they agree too. They can say no to any part, or stop at any time, and it will not affect anything at school.

Say yes online

  1. You enter your child’s details and give your permission.
  2. Your child reads a short explanation and signs to say they agree.
  3. If you both want to, you add a screenshot of the phone’s screen time.
