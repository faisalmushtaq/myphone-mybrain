You have been invited

Information for young people, parents and carers.

This page explains what happens next. You can read it together or on your own. You can ask us anything that is unclear.

If you are a young person

Take your time. Ask questions. You decide which parts feel right for you. The first step will be MySelf, a short survey about everyday life, wellbeing and digital technology.

If you are a parent or carer

Read the study information with your child. You can ask the research team questions before you make any decisions together.

What happens next

MySelf is the first step.

When MySelf is ready, your school or the research team will tell you how to use it. We will explain any future phone screen-capture data donation separately. It will always be optional.
