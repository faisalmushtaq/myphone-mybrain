Before you decide

Information for young people, parents and carers.

You may be reading this for yourself, or with a young person who has been invited. This page explains what happens next and where you can find help.

If you are a young person

You can take your time, ask questions and decide which parts feel right for you. The first participant step will be MySelf, a short survey about everyday life, wellbeing and digital technology.

If you are a parent or carer

You can read the study information with your child, ask the research team about anything that is unclear and discuss the choices together before consent is requested.

What happens next

MySelf comes first.

When MySelf is ready, your school or the research team will tell you how to access it. Any future phone screen-capture data donation will be introduced separately and will remain optional.
