Only if you choose to.

If the young person and their family agree, they send us screenshots of the phone’s Screen Time page (iPhone) or Digital Wellbeing page (Android). These show which apps were used and for how long.

They do not show messages, photos, videos, posts, contacts, or what was watched or searched. You see each screenshot before it is sent, and you can hide any part of it first. Screenshots are kept with a code instead of a name.

It is optional: saying no does not affect any other part of the study, and nothing changes at school.
