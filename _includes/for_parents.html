Share your child’s screen time.

Difficult questions and safeguarding

Some of the questions are about feelings and wellbeing, and a few young people may find them difficult or upsetting. Your child can skip any question, take a break or stop at any time, and a researcher is there to help.

If, during the session, your child tells us something, or something happens, that makes us worried about their safety or someone else’s, we pass it on to your school’s designated safeguarding lead, following the school’s safeguarding procedures. Survey answers are not read at the time and may not be looked at for a long time, so anyone who needs help should tell a researcher, a teacher or an adult they trust. More in the information sheet.
