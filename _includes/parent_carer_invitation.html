For parents and carers

Thank you for taking the time to look at this with your child.

Participation is a choice. Your child can ask questions, decide which parts feel right for them and stop at any time. Your school and the research team will share the information needed before any consent is requested.

The MySelf survey is the first participant step. A future phone screen-capture data-donation step will be optional. Choosing not to use it will not affect whether your child can take part in other parts of the study.
