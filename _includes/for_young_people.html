What next?

Want to take part?

Do it with your parent or carer: they say yes first, then you decide. It takes about 10 minutes.

Take part  →

Got a question?

Read the questions other young people ask, ask your teacher, or ask the researchers when they visit your school.

Questions from young people  → What happens at the session  →

Don’t want to take part?

That’s fine. You don’t need to fill anything in, and on the day you can say no to any part. Nobody will mind, and it won’t change anything at school.

If anything in the study worries or upsets you, talk to an adult you trust at home or at school, or call Childline free, any time, on 0800 1111.
