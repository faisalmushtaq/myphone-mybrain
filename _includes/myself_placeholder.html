Access is arranged through participating schools and the research team

MySelf uses access details supplied to invited participants.

  1. 1

    Check the information from your school or the research team for the survey link or access details.

  2. 2

    Read the survey information before you begin and ask a question if anything is unclear.

  3. 3

    If you do not have access details, contact the research team and select “Participant information”.
