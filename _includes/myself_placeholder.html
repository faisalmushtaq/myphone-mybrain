How to access MySelf.

Your school or the research team gives invited participants access details

Use the access details you have been given.

  1. 1

    Check the information from your school or the research team for the survey link or access details.

  2. 2

    Read the survey information before you start. Ask us if anything is unclear.

  3. 3

    If you do not have access details, contact us and choose “Taking part”.

We will introduce the optional phone screen-capture data donation separately when it is ready.
