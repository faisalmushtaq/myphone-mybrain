The details, for families, schools and data protection officers

  • Who is responsible: the University of Leeds is the data controller.
  • Legal basis: public task (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(e)); for special category data, such as health records and information about wellbeing, research in the public interest (Article 9(2)(j)). The study’s data protection impact assessment was signed off by the University in May 2026.
  • How long we keep it: research data for up to 10 years after the project ends, in line with University of Leeds research data guidance. Names and contact details are kept apart from research data.
  • Where it is kept: information given on this website, including screenshots, is stored for the University in Google Cloud’s London region. Linked records stay within Connected West Yorkshire’s secure systems.
  • Questions about your data or your rights: the University’s data protection team, dpo@leeds.ac.uk. About the study: {{ site.email }}.
