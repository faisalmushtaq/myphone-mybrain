Who can express interest?

Secondary schools in Bradford and Leeds.

Schools need a named contact who can discuss timetable, space, safeguarding and communication with families. An initial conversation is simply a chance to decide whether the project is a good fit.

What the team provides

Practical research delivery.

We bring the research team, equipment and session materials. We work with the school to plan a workable format, including information for staff, families and pupils.

What schools receive

Learning, information and a partnership.

Pupils encounter brain science, digital data and research methods. Schools receive clear participation materials and accessible updates as findings become ready to share.

The school participation process

From first conversation to follow-up.

Schools remain in control of whether to proceed at each stage. The exact timetable and session format are agreed together before any delivery is arranged.

  1. 01

    Talk it through

    Tell us about your school, year groups and questions. We explain what participation could involve.

  2. 02

    Plan the practical details

    Agree the school contact, suitable space, timetable, safeguarding route and family communication approach.

  3. 03

    Share information

    Staff, families and pupils receive the relevant information before any decision or consent is requested.

  4. 04

    Deliver and follow up

    The research team delivers the agreed session and keeps schools informed about relevant next steps and findings.
